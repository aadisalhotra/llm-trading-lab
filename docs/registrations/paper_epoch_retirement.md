## 1. The registered procedure (Research's text, verbatim)

> Paper-epoch state retirement (registered procedure). At the broker-paper cutover: (1) Series closure — each cohort book’s paper-epoch series terminates at its final paper session with its terminal equity recorded in the integrity ledger; the Phase A series is closed with a recorded endpoint, never truncated silently. (2) Read-only archive — the paper-epoch state files move intact to data/state_archive/paper_epoch/ (or equivalent), never deleted, never mutated; the final pre-archive state is pinned by commit hash in the ledger entry. (3) Scope — the five cohort books only; DeepSeek’s paper-only arm is excluded — its state remains live and its series continues uninterrupted. (4) Reproducibility invariant — post-archive, rebuilding any published Phase A month from its committed layer still works (the reproduce-May class regression runs against the archived state as its proof); the archive relocates state, it does not orphan the layer. (5) Guard semantics — InceptionEpochError is the enforcement mechanism and is satisfied by the archive step, never bypassed or suppressed; the fresh inception then proceeds per the capital registration. (6) Completion record — the human step emits a ledger entry on completion (per-book terminal values, archive path, commit hash, operator, date); per the undifferentiated-failure principle, a deliberate human step that completes silently is indistinguishable from one that didn’t happen.
